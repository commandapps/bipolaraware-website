The hard part

An episode is far easier to handle early. The early signs are the hardest to see from the inside.

By the time a shift is obvious, you've often lost the window where small adjustments do the most good. BipolarAware exists to give that window back — to notice the quiet drift in your sleep and rhythm while there's still plenty of room to act.

How it works

A quiet loop that runs on your behalf.

No daily homework. BipolarAware does the watching, asks little of you, and gets sharper the longer you use it.

It does this

It watches

In the background, it learns your normal — sleep timing, activity, the rhythm of your days — from signals your phone and watch already collect.

It does this

It flags, gently

When your patterns start to drift from your baseline, it gives you a calm, specific heads-up — never an alarm, never a verdict.

You do this

You decide

Glance and move on, add a ten-second check-in, loop in someone you trust, or take it to your clinician. You stay in charge of every step.

It does this

It learns you

Every day and every check-in makes the picture more yours. The longer you use it, the earlier and more accurately it sees.

What it watches

Signals you already give off — read for you.

Sleep and daily rhythm are among the earliest, most reliable tells of a coming shift. BipolarAware watches the passive ones for free, and lets you add the rest only if you want to.

Sleep

When you fall asleep and wake, and how steady that is — often the very first thing to move.

Activity

Movement and pace through the day — quiet stretches and restless surges both carry information.

Rhythm

The shape and regularity of your routine — when it loosens or tightens, it tends to mean something.

Your read

A quick check-in when you feel like sharing how today landed — it sharpens everything else.

Optional

Built to be trusted

You're letting an app watch your sleep. We don't take that lightly.

A tool you let this close has to earn it. We're building BipolarAware with clinicians, with privacy as the first requirement, and with a clear sense of what it is and isn't.

Yours, and private

Your signals are encrypted and belong to you. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone — clinician or loved one — without an action you take.

Made with clinicians

Designed alongside psychiatrists and people who live with bipolar, building on established research into early-warning signs and sleep.

Clear about its job

BipolarAware is an early-warning companion, not a diagnosis and not a doctor. When you need real clinical care, it points you toward it — calmly and clearly.

If you need help now

An app is never a substitute for a person.

If you're in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out right now — you deserve immediate, human support. In the U.S. you can call or text the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline any time, day or night. Anywhere else, your local emergency number or crisis line is the fastest way to reach someone.

BipolarAware is a wellness and early-awareness tool intended to support — not replace — professional medical care. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition, and its signals are not a clinical judgment.
